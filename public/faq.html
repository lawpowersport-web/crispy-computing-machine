Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know a box fits my vehicle?
Use the Year / Make / Model finder on the home or shop page. Each product also lists exact fitment (years and cab style) in its Vehicle Fitment tab. If your vehicle is selected, product pages show a green "Fits" message.
Which subwoofers work in underseat boxes?
Underseat boxes are designed for shallow-mount subwoofers. Check the mounting depth in the product Specs tab and compare it to your sub’s mounting depth.
How long does it take to build my box?
Every box is built to order in 3–5 business days. Custom designs take 7–10 business days.
How much is shipping?
We charge a flat ground rate in the continental US, and shipping is free on orders over the free-shipping threshold shown in the cart.
Is installation difficult?
Most boxes bolt in using factory seat-mounting points in under an hour with basic hand tools. Instructions are included.
What is your return policy?
Unused, uninstalled boxes can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund minus return shipping. Custom builds are final sale.
Is there a warranty?
Yes — a lifetime warranty against seam and joint failure under normal use.
How do you use my data?
We only use your information to process orders and, if you opt in, send newsletters. We never sell your data. Cookies keep your cart and preferences.
